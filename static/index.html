Ground track

The line is the satellite's ground track — the point on Earth directly beneath it — over the next ~110 minutes (about one orbit for a low-orbit satellite). The marker is its live position right now.

Upcoming passes next 7 days, from the selected ground station

A pass is a window when the satellite is above the horizon and could be tracked/contacted. Max elevation is how high it climbs in the sky (90° = straight overhead) — higher usually means a cleaner signal. Only passes reaching at least 10° are shown.
